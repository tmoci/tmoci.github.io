Institutional Mandate

The Museum of Contemporary Inquiry (TMOCI) is an independent international research and archival platform dedicated to long-term artistic inquiry, critical exhibition practices, and autonomous documentation.

Operating outside commercial gallery systems and market-driven validation, TMOCI establishes permanent conceptual ledgers and spatial research dossiers. By prioritizing intellectual intent over material ownership, the institution serves as a structural counter-weight to art market amnesia.

TMOCI collaborates with international scholars, institutions, and practitioners to support specialized projects, cross-disciplinary discourse, and open-access research. Its archive functions not as a passive repository, but as an active epistemological site—ensuring that uncompromising theoretical inquiry is preserved, analyzed, and sustained across global networks.
