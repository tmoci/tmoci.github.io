Institutional Mandate

The Museum of Contemporary Inquiry (TMOCI) serves as a transnational framework dedicated to long-term artistic inquiry, exhibition theory, and autonomous critical practice. The initiative operates to foster scholarly analysis and provide independent documentation across international cultural landscapes.

Prioritizing intellectual methodology and rigorous research over commercial imperatives, TMOCI provides an open structure for spatial and theoretical investigations. By documenting critical artistic developments and institutional histories, the museum maintains an accessible forum for curators, academics, and researchers.

Through international collaborations, published papers, and open-access research repositories, TMOCI actively engages with contemporary debates surrounding exhibition models, material culture, and artistic autonomy. Its archives exist to ensure that significant theoretical contributions remain documented and publicly accessible for global discourse.
