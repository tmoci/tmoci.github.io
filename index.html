The Museum of Contemporary Inquiry

TMOCI operates as an independent international research and archival platform dedicated to long-term artistic inquiry, critical exhibition practices, and autonomous documentation. Functioning outside commercial gallery systems, the institution establishes permanent conceptual ledgers and spatial research dossiers to support transnational discourse.
